
SEGMENTATION JUSTIFICATION
==========================

We defined three customer segments based on total debit spend (monetary),
using percentile-based thresholds to handle the highly right-skewed
distribution (mean = EGP 228K, median = EGP 95K, ratio = 2.4x).

Thresholds:
- VIP        = top 20%  (monetary >= 80th percentile)
- Mass       = middle 50% (monetary between 30th and 80th percentiles)
- Low-Value  = bottom 30% (monetary < 30th percentile)

Why percentiles? Fixed EGP cutoffs would break if new data shifts the
spending baseline. Percentiles adapt to the data distribution and ensure
stable segment sizes regardless of seasonal or macroeconomic changes.

The 20/50/30 split follows the Pareto principle: our VIPs (20% of customers)
drive 71% of total debit volume (EGP 774M out of EGP 1.08B), while
Low-Value customers (30% of the base) contribute only 4% of volume.
This makes the segments actionable for partnership scoring and BNPL targeting.

Validation: Despite segmenting on monetary alone, the groups show strong
separation on recency (VIP avg 13 days vs Low-Value 29 days), frequency
(VIP 35 txns vs Low-Value 14 txns), and outlier ratio (VIP 30% vs Low-Value 6%),
confirming that the tiers represent genuine behavioral archetypes, not
just arbitrary spend bins.
